# Out of Scope — MVP

These will NOT be built in the MVP.

- Multiple agent accounts or role management
- Customer-facing portal or ticket status tracking
- Mobile app or mobile-optimized UI
- Email attachment handling
- SLA enforcement or escalation timers
- Spam filtering or abuse prevention
- AI response feedback / rating system
- Automated ticket assignment rules
- Integrations with CRMs or third-party tools
- Tech stack decisions (decided separately)
- Knowledge base article management UI (MVP uses a single uploaded PDF/doc)
